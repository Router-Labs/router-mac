# Router for Mac

Private AI on your Mac. Local chat on your Mac, and sealed chat through Solrouter.

**Download:** [Router.dmg](https://github.com/Router-Labs/router-mac/releases/latest/download/Router.dmg) (latest release)

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. Open the DMG and drag Router to Applications.

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
